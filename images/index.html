About Us

A family bakery rooted in tradition since 1987.

Mphoentle Bakery was founded by Marie Dubois and her husband Paul in a small kitchen in the heart of the city. What started as a passion for perfect bread has grown into one of the most beloved bakeries in the community. Every loaf, pastry and cake is made from scratch each morning using locally sourced ingredients and time-honoured recipes passed down through generations.
🌾 Our Ingredients

We partner with local farms to source organic flour, free-range eggs, and seasonal fruit. Nothing artificial ever crosses our threshold — just honest, wholesome ingredients you can taste.
